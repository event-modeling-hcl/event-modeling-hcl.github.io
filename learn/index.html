Which track is yours?

  • You are new to Event Modeling: do Track 1 first. Then do Track 2.
  • You know Event Modeling and want the language: start Track 2 at Get started.
  • You want to see the tool work first: open Get started or the playground, then return to Track 1.

Both tracks use the same example: a pet clinic that schedules appointments and adds a weather forecast to each one. You meet the same Events, Commands, and Read Models in the method and in the code.

How to learn from these pages

Reading is not enough. You remember more when you try to answer first. Each page follows the same rhythm:

  1. Read the goals at the top. They tell you what you can do after the page.
  2. Answer each "Try first" question in your head. Then open the answer.
  3. Work through the "Check yourself" questions at the end. Do not scroll back while you answer.
  4. Wait two or three days. Then take the mixed review. A pause makes the memory stronger.
  5. Apply the idea to your own work. Model one small process from your job, and type it in the playground.

If you cannot answer a question, that is useful. Go back to that page, read the part again, and try the question once more.
